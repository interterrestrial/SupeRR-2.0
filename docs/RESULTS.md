## Qualification and uncertainty

All selections use eligible 2022–2026 evidence without recent-form weighting. An excluded format is not a zero rating. Every official player is **Boundary Top 10** under the rule requiring at least 75% Top-10 inclusion in both bootstrap modes for a Core classification. These are the locked full-data selections, with material uncertainty around their positions.

Source: the Phase 7A canonical player evidence and Phase 6 interpretation. Full player comparisons and uncertainty are in the [editable report](../final_submission/SupeRR_Selector_Methodology_Report.docx) and [Phase 6 report](../Phase%206%20Evidence%20Interpretation%20and%20Reporting%20Report.md).
